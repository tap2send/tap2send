Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

Welcome to Tap2Send. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and store your data when using our service, including integrations with WhatsApp Cloud API.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name, email address, phone number (when you create an account)
  • WhatsApp Business API details (Phone Number ID, WhatsApp Token)
  • Messages, templates, and contacts you save inside Tap2Send
  • Technical data such as IP address, device info, browser type

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide messaging services via WhatsApp Cloud API
  • To save and manage your message templates
  • To secure your account and prevent unauthorized access
  • To improve performance and user experience

3. WhatsApp Cloud API Usage

Tap2Send uses the official WhatsApp Cloud API. When you send messages, your content is delivered securely through WhatsApp servers. We do not share your data with any third party except WhatsApp API for message delivery.

4. Data Security

We use modern encryption and secure storage via Firebase to protect your data. Only you have access to your Firestore database collections.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your account or data
  • Update or modify your information
  • Stop using Tap2Send at any time

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with external companies. Your information is only shared with:

  • WhatsApp / Meta (only for message sending)
  • Firebase (for secure database storage)

7. Cookies

Tap2Send may use cookies for login authentication and performance improvements.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted here.
